Brand kit

Notate turns speech into text and keeps it on your device. Everything here follows from that: a sound wave becoming writing, one blue, one typeface made for reading transcripts.

The mark

A sound wave turns into two lines of text: speech in, writing out. White on a blue gradient, no outline, no text. Clear space equals the stroke width. Always blue.

Dot, the mascot

A little blue page with the icon's sound wave for hair and two lines of text on its body. Flat two-tone blue, no outline. Lives on the landing and in empty states, never in the app icon.
