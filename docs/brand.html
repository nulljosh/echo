Echo

Echo is named for the nymph who repeats every word. The app hears you and gives it back written down, on your device. Everything here follows from that.

01

The mark

A voice wave rings out, fades like an echo, and settles into a written line, over a shorter line. White on a blue gradient, round caps, no outline, no letters. Clear space equals the stroke. Always blue, never red.

02

1-bit art

Public domain paintings of Echo, the Muses and memory, ordered-dithered to one bit (a 4 by 4 Bayer matrix) and used as a mask, so the dots take the band's light blue over film grain. Each image is used once. Never a photo, never a gradient.

06

Controls

Solid Ghost

App controls are 48 point capsules; the record button is the one circle. Web buttons are square and tracked caps. Focus is a 3 point ring at 30 percent accent. Motion is 0.2 s ease and honours reduced motion.
